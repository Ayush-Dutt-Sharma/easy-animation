# Production script: "<video name>"

Status: draft. Not approved yet.

This is the shot-by-shot build sheet. The voice lines are locked in [brief.md](brief.md), and this file never changes their wording. [The parked 2:20 Uber script](../uber-live-map/parked/script-2m20.md) is a filled-in example.

**How to read it:**
- **Timecodes are estimates** until Phase 2 replaces them with real ones from `transcript.json`. Shots are tied to words, not seconds, so everything re-times on its own.
- **A shot starts on its first word.** The picture changes 2 frames before that word is spoken.
- **"On 'word'"** means the beat lands 2 frames before that word. Sound effects land on the frame of the thing they belong to.
- **Coordinates** are screen pixels, measured from the top-left corner.
- **Levels** are peak dBFS, relative to a voice that peaks around −6 dBFS.
