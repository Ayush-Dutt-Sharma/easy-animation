## 1. Script (locked once approved)

- One idea per sentence.
- Write numbers as words ("seven days", not "7 days").
- Add a pronunciation note for every brand name and acronym.

Sections depend on the kind of video ([storytelling.md](../../library/craft/storytelling.md)):
- **Ad:** hook, mechanism, results, proof, offer and urgency, guarantee and CTA.
- **Explainer:** long: hook, one example, zoom out, one section per point, takeaway, end card. Short (under 60 s): hook, numbered points, takeaway.
