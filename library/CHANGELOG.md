# Library changelog

Newest first. Each entry says what changed and which video or source taught it.

## 2026-09-28: v0.3, the style test

From building the Phase 1 shot in both looks ([style-test.html](../videos/uber-live-map/style-test.html)):
- **paper-pencil, watercolor-ink:** `--white` and `--subtitle` tokens, a `fine` line class for small details, and watercolour's line widths and wash method as built.
- **city-street:** the layout as built. The avenue is flat, the furniture stands at the curb, and there are fewer people. The road's `--dusk` reads purple in watercolour.

## 2026-09-27: v0.2, LinkedIn and your own voice

From moving the Uber explainer to a LinkedIn cut under 60 s:
- **format-9x16:** a feed-safe area, the middle 4:5 that LinkedIn's main feed shows on mobile. Badges move to y 400.
- **typography, mix, timing-reads:** subtitles are burned in by default on platforms that autoplay muted.
- **storytelling:** start from the platform; a short-explainer structure.
- **voice, pipeline:** recording your own voice is the default, with a how-to. ElevenLabs is the fallback.
- **Rule 7 fixes:** "?" bubbles for polling, icons on the state card, and a ✓ seal as the wordless COMMIT (data-graphics, labels-and-stamps, metaphors).
- **common-failures:** planning the length before the platform; words in the scene the voice never says.
- **Templates:** the brief asks for the platform first; the script template's example link points to the parked sheet.

## 2026-09-27: v0.1, the first split

- **From the Uber explainer:** its production bible (formerly Part A of its script) now lives in separate modules:
  - the theme, format, typography, camera and transitions;
  - animation numbers;
  - characters, sets and props;
  - voice, ambience, sound effects and mix.
- **From John Heibel's PDoomVideo and ClaudeAnimationBase:**
  - the goals, timing by reads, and the animation principles;
  - the common-failures list and model sheets;
  - how a face changes;
  - strips and crops in the review loop;
  - the thumbnail test;
  - a second theme, watercolor-ink.
- **New:**
  - the visual-metaphor dictionary for system explainers;
  - the retro that feeds this folder;
  - IDs for a few common sounds that had none: SFX-WINGS, SFX-CLOTH, SFX-STEP, SFX-BEEP, SFX-TOSS and SFX-RATTLE.
- Everything is `draft`, because nothing is built yet.
