# Set: system land

Status: draft · Source: the Uber script (shots 11–12, 17a–17b, 19–21, 23a–26) · Used in: uber-live-map · Updated: 2026-09-27

The paper world where systems live as buildings and machines. What each piece stands for is in [metaphors.md](../craft/metaphors.md). Light: `flat`, unless noted.

## Backdrop

- Light `--bluegrey`, with faint grid-paper lines. Once the viewer has entered it, they know they're "inside the system".
- **Diagram page:** clean grid paper (a faint `--bluegrey` 40 px grid) for pure diagrams. It's calm on purpose: nothing moves but the line boil.

## Warehouse: a write-heavy store

- A cutaway of connected bays (6 for a cluster), with tall shelves full of small hatched boxes.
- A conveyor enters from the top left. There's a big wall clock at the upper right and a counter sign at the top centre.
- **Always moving:**
  - 2 light shafts from skylights, with dust drifting in them;
  - the rollers spin and the boxes jiggle;
  - robots pass boxes hand to hand.
- Bed: AMB-WAREHOUSE.

## Toll gateway: an API gateway

- A toll booth in the centre, with a striped barrier arm and the gatekeeper in the window.
- Below it, lanes of incoming requests, with stripes scrolling toward the gate.
- A pie-chart sign on a pole, and a row of tiny phone icons along the bottom.
- Bed: AMB-GATE.

## Pneumatic tube: a persistent connection

- A glass tube from the backend to a phone.
  - It draws itself in (10 frames).
  - A glow runs once in each direction for the handshake.
- Arrows flow inside it.
- A label plate hangs on the tube and can flip over to a new label.
- The tube can widen (1.3×) and carry arrows both ways.
- A small desk by its mouth fits the flame clerk and a mini conveyor of event cards.

## Vault room: durable databases

- The vaults from [tech-mascots.md](../characters/tech-mascots.md), in a front cutaway: a stack of stone bricks inside, one per record, with new ones on top.
- A light shaft with dust motes.
- A stone-room reverb on the sound.

## Shelf display: a comparison

- Three wooden `--brown` shelves side by side (each 300×360 px, at y 650–1150), holding mini versions of the set pieces.
- A tech tag above each one, and a caption card below (y 1220).
- A warm spotlight cone lights one shelf at a time, and the others dim to 30%. Each mini piece keeps its own loop.
- Bed: AMB-PAPER.
