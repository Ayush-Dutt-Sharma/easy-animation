# Set: data center

Status: draft · Source: the Uber script (shots 8–11, 15b, 24) · Used in: uber-live-map · Updated: 2026-09-27

Where a service lives. Light: `server-room`. Bed: AMB-DC.

## Server room

- A dark room: `--night` mixed 60% with paper, hatched.
- **Far background:** rows of racks with LED dots that twinkle (seeded, every 1–3 s).
- **Midground:** a round platform with a **ring of 12 worker boxes** ([tech-mascots.md](../characters/tech-mascots.md)).
  - They sit on a circle (radius 330 px, centred at 540, 900), drawn as an ellipse at a 0.55 ratio.
  - A faint dashed circle joins them.
- **Floor:** vents, with about 20 slow dust particles rising from them.
- **Scale reveal:** pull back and the 12 turn out to be part of a ring of about 200 tiny workers (radius 420 px), with the original 12 still highlighted.
- **Orbit feel:** ease the ellipse from 0.55 to 0.60 and rotate the ring a few degrees while zooming.

## Memory chip (close-up)

- A `--sage`/`--olive` circuit board with `--gold` contacts, filling about 70% of the frame.
- A glowing window on it shows a mini map with moving dots.
- Tiny LEDs on the board blink.

## Exterior (cutaway)

- The building in cutaway, with the ring glowing and slowly turning behind a big round window.
- Vent steam rises from the roof.
- A hatch in the side wall can open onto a conveyor ([system-land.md](system-land.md)).
