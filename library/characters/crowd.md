# Crowd

Status: draft · Source: the Uber script · Used in: uber-live-map · Updated: 2026-09-27

Background people. They make a place feel alive and never pull focus from the lead.

- **Variants:** 6 sweater colours (`--sage`, `--sand`, `--bluegrey`, `--brown`, `--olive`, `--dusk`) and 3 hair shapes, with simple dot eyes. They have [Sam](sam.md)'s build, drawn with less detail.
- **Walk cycle:** 8 drawings on twos (0.53 s per cycle), at 60–90 px/s.
- **Placement:** start points, directions and speeds are seeded. On a street, some walk behind the lead, a few walk on the far sidewalk (drawn smaller), and one waits.
- **Props:** a coffee cup (they sip from it) and a tote bag. Add more as videos need them.
- **Reactions:** they can look up or turn toward an event one after another, 3 frames apart, never all together.
- **Far silhouettes:** flat shapes with no face, for passing behind things.
- **No twinning:** different speeds, phases and gaps.
